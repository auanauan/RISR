Welcome to RIS Ratchapruek

Welcome to RIS Ratchapruek

Ruamrudee International School, Ratchapruek Campus serves students in PreK2 to Grade 10. Following the American curriculum and in the tradition of Ruamrudee International School, founded in 1957, RISR builds a culture of care and inquiry that supports holistic development and individual achievement for each child.

Ruamrudee is Thai for “union of hearts,” which perfectly captures the school’s philosophy of welcoming all children into an environment of care and compassion. RIS embraces a progressive, innovative approach to education, emphasizing global citizenship and character development in a multicultural community.

    COMMUNITY

    To continue our legacy of success, in August of 2019, RIS opened a second campus in the Ratchapruek areas, offering outstanding elementary education from Pre-K 2–Grade 5. Grade 6 was added in 2020-2021, Grades 7-8 in 2021-2022, Grade 9 in 2022-2023 and Grade 10 in 2023-2024. Grade 11 will be added in 2024-2025 and by 2025-2026, RISR will celebrate its first graduating class.

    RATCHAPRUEK CAMPUS

    The RIS Ratchapruek Campus, situated in Nonthaburi, west of Bangkok, closely follows the philosophy of RIS Minburi. In all aspects of school life, whether through lessons or co-curricular programs, RIS Ratchapruek Campus allows students the opportunity to grow. Inspirational teachers and outstanding teaching and learning foster a culture of excellence, creativity and develop students’ ability to work confidently both independently and alongside others. At RISR, every student is a unique individual and the school celebrates the diversity of students from many cultures who learn together. Consistently recognized as an inclusive school, RISR promotes differentiated instruction and nurtures English language acquisition and development across the curriculum, while the Learning Support program provides students with the personalized accommodations and the support that they need to be successful.

    COMMUNITY & CAMPUS

    Ruamrudee International School was founded as Ruamrudee School in 1957. RIS has thousands of alumni in Thailand and around the world who continue to live by the school's motto, "Ad Astra," in their personal and professional lives. RIS Ratchapruek serves students from 15 different countries, who live in Nonthaburi, Bangkok, and the surrounding communities. Our campus currently serves students in PreK to Grade 9, with Grade 11 opening in August, 2024.
    RIS Ratchapruek opened in August 2019 in Bang Kruai, Nonthaburi. Our purpose-built campus is near Ratchapruek Road and approximately 20 kilometers north of Bangkok and west of the Chao Praya River. Our Secondary Building is currently under construction. The Secondary Building will be the home of our middle school and high school programs.

    WHY RIS RATCHAPRUEK?

    Care

    Our faculty, staff, and families are committed to creating an environment of care. Each child is seen as an individual and nurtured to grow and achieve according to our Principles of Phoenix, or "Head, Hands, and Heart," becoming balanced and successful individuals who can are equipped to become leaders.

    community

    Our school is a safe place for all to set goals, take risks, and grow through inquiry. Our approach to Student Support ensures that the academic, physical and social needs of students are met in an inclusive community where knowledge, skills, and values are nurtured together.

    compassion

    Students become leaders who show compassion through action, inspired by Ruamrudee International School's mission and vision and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

    connection

    RISR's connections with our Partners for the Future in Nonthaburi, Bangkok, throughout Thailand, and beyond ensure that our students can connect their learning to real organizations, companies, and opportunities to create change in Thailand and the world.
